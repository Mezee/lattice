## Phonon (Gradium)
Speech is generated by [Phonon](https://github.com/gradium-ai/xn-ptts) through its `ptts` command, which this app
calls but does not bundle. The runtime is MIT OR Apache-2.0. The default model (`phonon-31m-en`), its tokenizer and
voices are Apache-2.0. Phonon builds on Pocket TTS and the Mimi codec from Kyutai. Gradium asks that voices not be
used to impersonate real people or to deceive listeners. The multilingual `phonon-40m` models are CC BY-NC 4.0
(non-commercial) and are not used by default.
